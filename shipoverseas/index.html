    API-READY FEATURES

    What ShipOverseas can support

    Container Tracking API

    Track loaded, departed, arrived, discharged, and gate-out events by container or bill of lading.

     ETA and ETD Alerts

    Warn customers and operations teams when a sailing or arrival changes from the planned schedule.

     Detention and Demurrage

    Flag fees risk when containers stay too long at terminal or after free days.

     Rolled Container

    Show when a container missed the planned vessel and is moved to a later sailing.

     Pick-up Notice

    Notify the receiver when cargo is ready for release at the destination terminal.

     Email Updates

    Every status change creates a customer email record and can connect to a real email provider.
